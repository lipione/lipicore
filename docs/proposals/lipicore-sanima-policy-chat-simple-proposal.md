---

## 1. Proposal Summary

Lipi One proposes a simple Phase 1 rollout of **LipiCore Policy Assistant** for Sanima Bank.

The goal is to help Sanima Bank staff quickly find trusted answers from approved internal policies, SOPs, circulars, manuals, and procedures. Phase 1 also includes our internal staff chat system so teams can coordinate within the same secured environment.

This is intentionally not a large enterprise AI rollout. It is a focused onboarding package: policy retrieval first, internal chat first, and future upgrades only after Sanima Bank sees practical value.

**Phase 1 price: NPR 250,000 per month + VAT.**

---

## 2. What Sanima Bank Gets

| Area | Included in Phase 1 |
|---|---|
| Policy questions | Staff can ask natural questions in English or Nepali. |
| Approved knowledge | Answers are generated from approved bank documents only. |
| RAG retrieval | Semantic search, keyword search, hybrid retrieval, and reranking. |
| Source evidence | Answers show source passages and document references. |
| Page and clause references | PDF page, printed page, heading, clause, section, or paragraph shown where available. |
| Trust behavior | If the answer is not found in approved documents, the system says so instead of guessing. |
| Staff assistance | Light summaries and small internal email/reply drafts based on approved documents. |
| Internal chat | Direct messages, department channels, groups, announcements, and file/image sharing subject to policy. |
| Desktop app | Windows and macOS software connected to the approved hosted LipiCore environment. |
| Admin controls | User roles, department access, document approval, audit logs, and basic usage visibility. |

---

## 3. How It Helps the Bank

- Reduces time spent searching for policies, SOPs, and circulars.
- Helps staff locate the correct document and source faster.
- Gives source-backed answers with page and clause references where available.
- Reduces dependency on senior staff for routine policy lookup.
- Keeps official interpretation and final decisions with authorized bank staff.
- Gives Sanima Bank a low-risk starting point for enterprise AI adoption.
- Adds internal staff chat without requiring a separate communication tool.

## 6. Not Included in Phase 1

To keep the first rollout simple and affordable, Phase 1 does not include:

| Not Included Now | Reason |
|---|---|
| Full enterprise analytics model | Keep Phase 1 focused on policy retrieval. |
| Heavy OCR or bulk scanned archive processing | Can be scoped later after document review. |
| Core banking, DMS, SharePoint, email, or intranet integration | Avoid integration complexity in first rollout. |
| Automated compliance monitoring or custom reporting | Future module after policy retrieval adoption. |
| Department-specific AI agents or API integrations | Future expansion only if Sanima Bank requests it. |
| Automatic credit, risk, legal, regulatory, or customer decisions | Final decisions remain with authorized bank staff. |
| Fine-tuning LipiLLM on Sanima Bank data | Not included unless separately approved in writing. |
