# LipiCore Bank Demo Script

- **Prepared:** May 23, 2026
- **Demo theme:** Bank Staff AI Helpdesk
- **Audience:** Customer care, branch operations, compliance/helpdesk, product/policy, IT, risk, procurement

## Demo Opening

Use this opening:

> LipiCore is not an autonomous decision-maker. It is a secure internal AI assistant for bank staff. It runs inside bank-controlled infrastructure and helps staff answer from approved documents, analyze internal files, summarize, and draft responses with source evidence and audit logs.

## Flow 1: Ask BankAi

**Purpose:** Show the general staff assistant mode.

1. Ask a customer-care or branch question such as: "How should staff respond when a customer reports a failed ATM transaction?"
2. Show that LipiCore searches approved knowledge when available.
3. Show source cards when a source matches.
4. If the answer uses general guidance, point out that it is not presented as approved bank policy.
5. Show staff-ready draft language and explain that staff can edit before sending.

**Buyer message:** Faster routine answers, better consistency, and less dependency on senior staff for common policy/product lookup.

## Flow 2: Ask Approved Knowledge

**Purpose:** Show strict source-backed mode for policy/compliance questions.

1. Ask a policy question that exists in approved documents.
2. Open the source evidence panel.
3. Show document title, page/section/chunk, source passage, and citation status.
4. Ask a question that is not covered by approved documents.
5. Show the refusal: LipiCore should say it cannot find support in approved documents.

**Buyer message:** This is the safe mode for policy, circular, SOP, compliance, and internal procedure questions.

## Flow 3: Analyze Internal File

**Purpose:** Show document upload and staff file analysis.

1. Upload a supported file such as a policy PDF, circular, FAQ, or customer case note.
2. Show ingestion status: queued, extracting/OCR, chunking, embedding, indexing, ready.
3. Ask for a summary, open questions, key obligations, or customer reply draft.
4. Show that the output is tied to the uploaded file where sources are available.
5. Explain that complex scans, tables, stamps, signatures, and handwritten notes require confidence review before high-risk use.

**Buyer message:** Staff can analyze internal files without sending them to external AI providers.

## Flow 4: Queue Long-Document Analysis

**Purpose:** Show the safe path for large PDFs, OCR-heavy files, and detailed Excel review.

1. Open the Document Library with a ready or approved PDF/XLS document.
2. Click `Queue analysis`.
3. Use a prompt such as: "Summarize key obligations, exceptions, dates, tables, and staff action points. Cite page or sheet labels where possible."
4. Show the status chip moving through queued, processing, packing context, generating, or completed.
5. Open the stored result and explain that staff review remains required for high-risk use.

**Buyer message:** Heavy documents do not block normal chat. LipiCore queues the work, uses extracted source excerpts, stores the result, and keeps final decisions with staff.

## Optional Flow 5: Controlled Decision Support

**Purpose:** Show that lending and compliance are handled as staff workflows, not autonomous decisions.

1. Open Support Desk, Compliance Workspace, or Loan Support.
2. Create a case or review record using approved source documents.
3. Generate or paste a staff draft, summary, checklist, risk note, or credit memo draft.
4. Point out the human review language and the absence of auto-approve/auto-reject behavior.

**Buyer message:** LipiCore can support regulated workflows with notes, drafts, and review records while final authority remains with bank officers.

## Close

Use this close:

> The first pilot should focus on customer care and branch support: 20-50 users, 200-500 approved documents, and a 30-day evaluation. We will measure answer accuracy, citation usefulness, response time, staff acceptance, and escalation reduction.
