# What LipiCore Does Not Do

- **Prepared:** May 23, 2026
- **Use for:** Risk, compliance, procurement, and executive review

LipiCore is a secure internal AI assistant and decision-support appliance for bank staff. It helps staff search approved bank knowledge, analyze internal files, summarize, compare, extract, and draft responses. It is not an autonomous bank decision engine.

## Final Decisions Stay With Bank Staff

LipiCore does not make final lending, compliance, regulatory, operational, or customer-impact decisions. It can prepare document summaries, OCR text extraction, checklists, customer-care drafts, policy explanations, and compliance notes, but authorized bank staff must review and approve final actions.

## It Does Not Replace Compliance Officers

LipiCore can reduce lookup, drafting, and circular review workload. It does not replace compliance officers, policy owners, supervisors, auditors, legal reviewers, or credit committees. Human interpretation and sign-off remain part of the bank's governance process.

## It Does Not Guarantee Regulatory Correctness

LipiCore answers from approved source documents and shows citations, including heading, clause, PDF page, and printed page where available for policy-like answers. Regulatory correctness depends on the bank keeping policies, circulars, SOPs, and product documents accurate, current, approved, and available in the knowledge base.

Citation completeness is not a legal guarantee. It means the required citation fields are present; staff still need to inspect the cited passage, document status, version state, and effective dates.

## It Does Not Perfectly Understand Every Document

LipiCore supports OCR, table extraction, document parsing, and queued long-document analysis, but scanned PDFs, complex tables, charts, seals, signatures, stamps, blurry pages, handwritten notes, and mixed-layout documents can require staff review. Low-confidence extraction should be verified before use in high-risk workflows.

## Large Documents Are Queued, Not Instant

Large PDFs, OCR-heavy files, and detailed Excel workbooks run as background analysis jobs. LipiCore can store the result and show job status, but turnaround depends on file quality, page count, OCR requirements, worker capacity, and deep-model queue load.

## Current Safe Large-File Statement

LipiCore can queue large-file analysis, select relevant extracted excerpts, and generate staff-reviewable summaries or action notes. It should not be described as perfect whole-document reasoning, perfect OCR, or automatic verification of signatures, seals, handwritten notes, or complex tables.

## Internal Workspace Tools Are Operational Aids

Employee Search, Notifications & Alerts, CEO's Message, Staff Inbox, Forex/Time/Dates, knowledge-gap tracking, policy-change watch, audit evidence packs, and banking workflow helpers are feature-flagged operational tools. They do not replace maker-checker controls, compliance sign-off, customer-impact approval, or Treasury ownership of exchange-rate updates.

## SLA Depends On Deployment Architecture

LipiCore can be deployed as a pilot, department rollout, or whole-bank high-availability architecture. Availability targets depend on the selected infrastructure, monitoring, backup/restore process, failover design, and support contract.

## It Is Not Sold As A Better General Model Than GPT-4/GPT-5

LipiCore's advantage is private bank deployment, bank-owned knowledge, source control, audit logs, and no external data exposure. Public frontier models may be stronger at general open-ended reasoning. LipiCore should be evaluated on bank-specific knowledge accuracy, citation usefulness, privacy, governance, and deployment fit.

## Approved Boundary Statement

LipiCore is a secure AI decision-support and staff-productivity system. It helps bank staff find, understand, summarize, and draft from approved bank knowledge while keeping humans responsible for final decisions.
